About

Small team. Long-term software.

Your Studio is an independent developer publishing on the Apple App Store. We build focused apps that do one job well, ship regular updates, and answer every support request ourselves.

We believe software should be fast, accessible, and respectful of the person using it. That means native frameworks, no dark patterns, clear pricing, and privacy by default. You will never find hidden data collection or advertising trackers in our apps.

Have a question or an idea? We read every message. Visit our support page to reach us.

How We Work

Four commitments in every release

  • Privacy by default

    We collect the minimum data needed to run the app. Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what and why, in plain language.

  • Native and fast

    Built with Swift and SwiftUI, tuned for each platform, and tested across the devices and OS versions we support.

  • Accessible to everyone

    VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, and keyboard navigation are part of the design, not an afterthought.

  • Real human support

    Questions are answered by the developers who wrote the code, typically within 2 business days.
