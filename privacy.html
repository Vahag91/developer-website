The short version

  • Our apps are designed to work without collecting personal information. Most data stays on your device or in your private iCloud account.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not show third-party advertising.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment card details.
  • Anonymous crash reports and usage statistics are shared with us only if you have opted in through Apple’s settings or in the app.
  • You can contact us at any time to access, correct, or delete your data.

1. Overview

We build apps with privacy as a design requirement. This Privacy Policy explains what information our apps and website may collect, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices available to you. It applies to every application we publish on the Apple App Store, Mac App Store, and related storefronts (collectively, the “Apps”), and to this website (the “Site”).

By using our Apps or Site, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use our Apps or Site. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in our Terms of Use.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

Depending on the App, you may choose to enter content such as notes, tasks, measurements, preferences, or other data needed for the App to function. This content is stored locally on your device and, if you enable it, synchronized through your personal iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit. We do not have access to data stored in your private iCloud database.

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you include in your message, such as your name, email address, device model, operating system version, and a description of the issue.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Diagnostics and crash reports. If you have enabled “Share with App Developers” in your device’s Analytics settings, Apple provides us with anonymized crash logs and performance statistics. These reports do not identify you personally.
  • Usage analytics. Some Apps may collect aggregate, non-identifying usage statistics (for example, which features are used most) to help us improve the product. Where this applies, it is disclosed in the App’s App Store privacy label and can be disabled in the App’s settings.
  • Device information. Basic technical details such as device type, OS version, language, and app version may be processed in order to deliver features, resolve compatibility issues, and respond to support requests.

2.3 Purchase information

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive a receipt confirming the transaction and the type of product purchased, but we never receive your name, billing address, or payment card details. Apple’s handling of your payment data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

2.4 Permissions and sensitive data

Some Apps request access to device capabilities such as Camera, Photos, Location, Notifications, or Health data. Access is requested only when needed for a feature you choose to use, is explained at the time of the request, and can be revoked at any time in your device Settings. Health data accessed through HealthKit is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is never sold or shared with third parties, in accordance with Apple’s HealthKit guidelines.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Apps and their features;
  • Synchronize your content across your devices when you enable iCloud;
  • Validate purchases and restore subscriptions you are entitled to;
  • Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve performance and usability;
  • Respond to your support requests and communicate about your account or purchases;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

We do not use your information for behavioral advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

4. Legal bases for processing

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract – to deliver the Apps and services you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR);
  • Legitimate interests – to secure, debug, and improve our Apps, and to respond to support requests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR);
  • Consent – for optional analytics, notifications, and access to device permissions, which you may withdraw at any time (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR);
  • Legal obligation – where processing is required by applicable law (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

5. Sharing and disclosure

We share information only in the limited circumstances below:

  • Service providers. With vendors that process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality obligations, such as Apple (App Store, iCloud, TestFlight) and, where disclosed for a specific App, crash-reporting or analytics providers.
  • Legal requirements. When required to comply with a law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or us.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any change in ownership or in the use of your personal information.
  • With your direction. When you ask us to share information, for example by using a share or export feature within an App.

7. Data retention

Content you create in an App remains on your device and in your iCloud account for as long as you keep it; deleting the App or the content removes it from your device, and iCloud data is removed according to Apple’s retention rules. Support correspondence is retained for up to 24 months after the request is resolved so we can follow up on related issues. Anonymized diagnostics are retained only as long as needed to analyze and fix the issue, and in any event no longer than 12 months.

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect information, including Apple’s on-device data protection, encrypted transport (TLS) for any network communication, encrypted iCloud storage, and access controls limited to the people who need it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the relevant authorities of any breach as required by law.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Delete your personal information;
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Receive your information in a portable format;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than those permitted by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@yourstudio.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally 30 days, or 45 days for California requests).

10. Deleting your data

Because our Apps store your content on your device and in your personal iCloud account, you are in control of it at all times:

  • On your device: delete the App from your Home Screen to remove all locally stored data.
  • In iCloud: open Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, select the App, and choose “Delete Data from iCloud”.
  • In-app account: if an App offers an account, it also offers an in-app option to delete that account and all associated data, as required by the App Store Review Guidelines.
  • Support records: email us and we will delete your support correspondence on request.

11. Children’s privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If an App is placed in the App Store Kids Category, it complies with Apple’s additional requirements for that category and does not include third-party analytics or advertising. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. International data transfers

We are based in United States. Any information we receive (for example, a support email) may be processed in that country and in other countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses to protect information transferred outside your jurisdiction.

13. This website

This Site is a static informational website. It does not use cookies, does not run third-party analytics or advertising scripts, and does not collect personal information unless you choose to contact us. Our web host may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps) for security and operational purposes, which are retained for a limited period.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Apps, legal requirements, or data practices. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page and, for material changes, provide notice within the affected App or on this Site. Your continued use of the Apps after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.
