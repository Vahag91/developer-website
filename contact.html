Before You Write

Help us help you faster

Including a few details in your first message lets us reproduce the issue and reply with a fix instead of a follow-up question.

  • The name of the app and its version (found in the app’s Settings or About screen)
  • Your device model and OS version (for example “iPhone 16 Pro, iOS 19.1”)
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead
  • Steps to reproduce the problem, if you know them
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if relevant
  • For purchase issues: the date of purchase (we never need your payment details)

Common Questions

Quick answers

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, then open the app’s settings and tap “Restore Purchases”. Subscriptions and one-time purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not your device.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your device, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select the app, and choose Cancel. You will keep access until the end of the current billing period.

How do I request a refund?

Apple processes all refunds for App Store purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase. We are not able to issue refunds directly, but let us know if we can help with anything.

Why isn’t my data syncing between devices?

Check that iCloud Drive is enabled on every device, that the app is allowed to use iCloud in Settings → your name → iCloud, and that both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID and connected to the internet. If it still does not sync, email us the details and we will investigate.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove iCloud data, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, select the app, and choose Delete Data. Full instructions are in our Privacy Policy.
